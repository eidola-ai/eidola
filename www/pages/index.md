# Eidola

## Intelligence should be intimate

The most useful AI is the one that knows you best — your habits, your work, your conversations, the shape of your days. That knowledge is powerful and deeply personal. It should stay under your control, and yours alone.

Most AI tools ask you to trade that intimacy for capability — to trust that whoever runs the model will handle what they learn about you well. Eidola is built on the opposite premise: that you should have to trust us with as little as possible, and be able to verify what's left. Your data lives on your device. When a request needs computation only a remote machine can do, it runs inside confidential-compute hardware your client re-verifies on every connection, sealed against us and the operators who run the infrastructure. What can be enforced by the architecture is; the rest is open, reproducible code you can read for yourself.

## In early development

Eidola is being built in the open. You can read every line, reproduce every build, and decide for yourself: start with [how to think about Eidola](/docs/paradigm/), read the [privacy guarantees](/docs/privacy-guarantees/) — the contract a named engineer signs against every release — or go straight to [the source](https://github.com/eidola-ai/eidola).
