# Payment complete

Checkout is done — thank you.

Your credits land on your account once our payment processor confirms the charge. That is usually a matter of seconds, but it happens on its own schedule rather than this page's, and nothing is lost if you leave first.

**You can close this window and go back to Eidola.** The app shows the new balance the next time it looks; Settings ▸ Account has a Refresh if you would rather not wait.

This page can't show you an order summary, and that is deliberate: your Eidola account carries no name or email, and the payment details you just entered stayed with our payment processor. Your receipt comes from them, by email.

Credits from a one-time purchase expire one year after purchase; credits granted by a subscription expire at the end of each billing period. Unused, unexpired credits are refundable on request — see the [terms](/terms/).
