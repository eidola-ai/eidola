# Billing

You've left the billing portal.

Whatever you did there — updated a card, changed or canceled a subscription, collected a receipt — is recorded with our payment processor, which is where that information lives. Eidola reads your subscription from them each time you open Settings ▸ Account, so the app catches up on its own.

**You can close this window and go back to Eidola.**
