# Privacy policy

The formal privacy policy for Eidola's hosted service is being prepared and will be published here before general availability.

Eidola's privacy commitments are architectural, not just contractual: the enforceable contract is the [privacy guarantees](/docs/privacy-guarantees/) document — enumerated, durable commitments that a named release engineer signs against every release, and that you can verify against the running code. The policy published here will restate those guarantees in conventional legal form; it will not weaken them.

Until the policy is published, the [privacy guarantees](/docs/privacy-guarantees/) and the [threat model](/docs/threat-model/) are the authoritative description of how Eidola handles your data.
